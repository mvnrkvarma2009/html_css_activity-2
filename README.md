## 🌟 Overview

**AETHER CHRONO-X** is a luxury, high-performance promotional website for a futuristic neural-interfaced spatial computing smartwatch.

Engineered with an **Industrial Luxury & Liquid Glass** design philosophy, it features:
- **Originkit ArgentCloudburst Shader:** WebGL2 digital rain curtain with interactive pointer parting, wind lean spring dynamics, and click-triggered lightning bolts.
- **Interactive Color Studio:** Live chassis and band finish switcher between *Cosmic Obsidian*, *Stellar Titanium*, and *Polar Lavender*.
- **Hardware Specifications Matrix:** Tabbed engineering breakdown (Chassis & Display, Neural Core, Biometric Sensors, Battery & Comms).
- **Customer & Critic Reviews:** 4.9/5 star ratings and reviews from major publications.
- **Dynamic Pre-Order Customizer:** Real-time price calculator based on case size, connectivity, and band material, with a confirmation order receipt modal.
